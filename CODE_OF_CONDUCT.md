# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing respect and consideration for others.
- Being constructive and welcoming when offering feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary remarks or actions.
- Violent, sexual, or hateful language or imagery in any public spaces related to the project.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples of representing the project include discussing the project on social media, in public presentations, or in other online or offline forums when using the project name or branding.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a new issue in this repository titled "Code of Conduct Incident" and including as much detail as is comfortable. If you prefer to keep the report private, you may also contact the repository owner directly at their GitHub profile: https://github.com/aromal-a

All reports will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainers may choose to:

- Request clarification or additional information.
- Temporarily or permanently remove privileges from the repository (for example, removing write access).
- Request that the contributor cease the behavior, or in severe cases, request removal from the community.

Reports will be handled with discretion to protect the privacy of everyone involved.
